# Employee Handbook

## Leave Policy

All full-time employees are entitled to 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave requests must be submitted at least 2 weeks in advance through the HR portal. Unused leave can be carried forward up to 5 days into the next year.

Sick leave: Employees may take up to 10 paid sick days per year with a medical certificate required for absences longer than 3 consecutive days.

Parental leave: 12 weeks of paid parental leave is available for new parents, to be taken within 12 months of birth or adoption.

## Expense Reimbursement

Business expenses up to $500 can be submitted through the expense portal with a receipt. Expenses over $500 require manager approval. Travel expenses must follow the company travel policy (economy class flights, standard hotel rates).

Meal allowance: $60 per day for business travel.

## IT Support

IT support is available Monday-Friday, 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM ET. Submit tickets at support@company.com or call ext. 4357. Critical system outages can be reported 24/7 via the emergency line at ext. 9111.

Password reset: Use the self-service portal at https://it.company.com/reset or contact the helpdesk.

## Remote Work

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval. Remote work agreements must be on file with HR. Employees working remotely must have a secure internet connection and a dedicated workspace.
