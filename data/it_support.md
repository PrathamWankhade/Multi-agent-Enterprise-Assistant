# IT Support — Approved Troubleshooting Guide

## IT Support Hours and Contacts

IT support is available Monday-Friday, 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM ET. Submit tickets at
support@company.com or call ext. 4311. Critical outages can be reported 24/7 via
the emergency line at ext. 9111.

## Laptop Boot Failure / Blue Screen

1. Hold the power button for 10 seconds, then restart.
2. Disconnect all external devices (docks, USB drives, monitors) and retry.
3. Try entering Safe Mode: hold Shift while selecting Restart.
4. If the device still fails to boot, stop and create an IT service request.
   Do not attempt repairs yourself.

## VPN Connection Problems

1. Confirm you are connected to the internet.
2. Sign out of the VPN client and sign back in with your SSO credentials.
3. Check that the VPN client is updated to the latest version.
4. Try a different network (e.g. mobile hotspot) to isolate the issue.
5. If the VPN still fails, create an IT service request with your location and
   the exact error message.

## Password Reset / Account Access

1. Use the self-service portal at https://password.company.com to reset your
   password.
2. If your account is locked, wait 15 minutes and try again.
3. For SSO failures, clear browser cookies and retry in a private window.
4. If access is still blocked, create an IT service request.

## Printer Problems

1. Check that the printer has paper and is online.
2. Clear any paper jam following the label inside the tray.
3. Restart the print spooler service or reboot your machine.
4. If the issue persists, create an IT service request with the printer name
   and floor location.

## Software Installation Requests

Software must be approved by IT before installation. Do not install
unlicensed software. Create a service request with the software name, version,
and business justification; approved requests are routed to the Software team.

## Escalation and Priorities

- Critical: service outage affecting many users — call ext. 9111 immediately.
- High: employee cannot work or has an imminent deadline.
- Medium: degraded functionality with a workaround.
- Low: minor or cosmetic issue.

All service requests require administrator approval before a technician is assigned.
